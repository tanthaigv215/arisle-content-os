# ARISLE Content OS

Version: 1.0

---

# Product Vision

ARISLE Content OS is an AI-powered content operating system designed to help the ARISLE marketing team plan, create, manage and optimize content across every marketing channel while ensuring every output follows the ARISLE brand identity.

---

# Project Goal

Build the best AI Marketing Operating System for ARISLE.

---

- Marketing Team
- Content Creator
- Designer
- Marketing Manager
- Founder

# Core Modules

## 1. Dashboard
The central workspace showing business insights, recent projects, AI shortcuts and team productivity.

---

## 2. AI Content Studio
Generate premium marketing content for Facebook, Website, Shopify, Email and Advertising.

---

## 3. Brand Brain
The central intelligence of ARISLE.

Contains:

- Brand Guidelines
- Customer Personas
- Product Knowledge
- Tone of Voice
- SEO Frameworks
- Marketing Frameworks
- Writing Standards

Every AI Agent must use Brand Brain before generating content.

---

## 4. Product Library
Manage all ARISLE products including descriptions, collections, materials, colors, images and selling points.

---

## 5. Campaign Manager
Manage marketing campaigns, objectives, target audiences and content strategy.

---

## 6. Content Calendar
Plan, organize and publish content across all marketing channels.

---

## 7. Analytics
Measure content performance and marketing effectiveness.

---

## 8. Settings
Manage users, AI models, API Keys and system configuration.

# Business Goals

The primary objective of ARISLE Content OS is to increase the productivity and consistency of the marketing team through AI.

## Objectives

- Reduce content creation time by at least 80%.
- Ensure every piece of content follows ARISLE Brand Guidelines.
- Centralize all marketing knowledge in one place.
- Eliminate repetitive manual work.
- Improve SEO quality across all website content.
- Increase the consistency of Facebook, Shopify and Website content.
- Build a reusable marketing knowledge system.
- Scale content production without increasing team size.

## Success Metrics

- Content production time
- Number of contents generated
- SEO quality score
- Brand consistency score
- Publishing frequency
- Team productivity
